# Area: Math

Math problems have a short numeric answer solvable with paper and pen in at most 10 minutes: counting, number theory, probability, geometry, sequences. No calculator is needed.

## Difficulty

- 3: the only daily band for Math; one or two clear ideas, with arithmetic that stays manageable.
- 5: see the deep problem notes when provided.

## Pitfalls

- Ambiguous wording: say whether ranges are inclusive, whether order matters, and what form the answer takes (integer, fraction as decimal with `tolerance`).
- Answers that need outside knowledge: do not assume named theorems beyond school math.

The `solver` must compute the answer by brute force or direct computation, independently of the solution text.
