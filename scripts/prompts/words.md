# Area: Words

Words problems are about letters and language: anagrams, word ladders, "what connects these", and light cryptic clues. Answers are one real word or a short phrase made of real words.

## Difficulty

- 1: the only band for Words; a clean, fair puzzle a newcomer can solve in a couple of minutes with the hints.

## Pitfalls

- Ambiguous wording: if two different words fit the clue, tighten the clue or list both in `accepted`.
- Answers that need outside knowledge (brand names, trivia, other languages): use only common English words.
