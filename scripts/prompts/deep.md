# Deep problems (difficulty 5)

A deep problem is a multi-step problem that rewards an hour of focused work, once a week. It should not fall to one trick.

- It has at least three stages, where each result feeds the next.
- The first step is approachable, so the player can begin, and the full answer still takes sustained effort.
- The final answer is checkable: one number, one word, or tests for code.
- The five hints are questions that open up the stages in order, and none of them states a result.
- The solution walks through every stage and explains why the shortcut attempts fail.
