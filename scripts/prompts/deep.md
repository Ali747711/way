# Deep problems (difficulty 5)

A deep problem is a multi-step problem that rewards an hour of focused work, once a week. It should not fall to one trick.

## Difficulty

- 5: the weekly band only; about an hour of focused work with paper and pen (or an editor for code), built from several ideas that each take real effort. It is harder than any daily problem in its area and still needs no outside knowledge.

## Shape

- It has at least three stages, where each result feeds the next.
- The first step is approachable, so the player can begin, and the full answer still takes sustained effort.
- The final answer is checkable: one number, one word, or tests for code.
- The five hints are questions that open up the stages in order, and none of them states a result.
- The solution walks through every stage and explains why the shortcut attempts fail.

## Pitfalls

- A hidden shortcut: if one clever observation or a quick brute force by hand collapses every stage, the problem is not deep; make each stage necessary.
- An answer that is hard to check: a final answer with several parts or a loose format invites correct work marked wrong; ask for one number or one word and say exactly what form it takes.
